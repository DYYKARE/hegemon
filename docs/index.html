Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: July 17, 2026

This policy applies to the Hegemon mobile game (package: com.yusuf.hegemon).

Data Collection

Hegemon does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • The game runs fully offline; no data is transmitted over the internet during play.
  • Your game progress (save file, settings) is kept only on your own device in the app's local storage, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • No account, login, location, identity, or device information is requested.
  • The game contains no ads and uses no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Since Hegemon collects no personal data, no data from children is collected either.
